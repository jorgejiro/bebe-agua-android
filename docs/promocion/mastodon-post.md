# Mastodon thread — Drink Water! / ¡Bebe agua!

Two versions of the same thread: English and Spanish. Publish each one as a thread (reply to your
own post), not as a single long post.

Notes for publishing:

- Every post fits in **500 characters**, the default limit on most instances. Mastodon counts every
  link as 23 characters, whatever its real length.
- Mastodon does not render Markdown: copy the text inside each code block exactly as it is.
- Attach up to 4 screenshots to the **first post** from
  `docs/store-assets/capturas/<lang>/telefono/`, and always add alt text. On Mastodon, images
  without a description get far fewer boosts. Suggested alt texts are included below.
- Hashtags are how people find posts on Mastodon, because there is no algorithmic feed. Keep them in
  the first post (discovery) and in the last post (topic). Use CamelCase so that screen readers can
  read them.
- Pin the first post to your profile.

## English

### Post 1/5

```
I couldn't find a water reminder app that fit what I needed, so I built my own. 💧

Drink Water! is a minimal Android water tracker:
• one tap to log a glass
• reminders only within your hours
• they stop once you reach your goal
• they vibrate instead of ringing

No account, no cloud, no ads, no trackers. And it's open source (MIT).

🧵 1/5

#Android #OpenSource #FOSS #Hydration
```

### Post 2/5

```
Why build another one? Every app I tried had at least one of these:

– ads, upsells or a mandatory account
– tips feeds, mascots, hearts and other gamification
– reminders outside my waking hours
– more nagging after I'd already hit my goal
– a reminder 5 minutes after I'd just had a drink
– a ringtone instead of a vibration

I only wanted to drink more water, quietly.

2/5
```

### Post 3/5

```
What it does:

💧 A big ring with drunk / goal, and a button that logs your usual amount
⏰ You pick the window and how many reminders per day; they're spread evenly
⏭️ Logging water pushes the next reminder forward
🔔 "Drink 200 ml" and "Snooze 15 min" right from the notification
🏠 A 1x1 widget: one tap and it's logged
📊 30-day history, average and streak
🎨 Material You, dark theme, EN/ES

3/5
```

### Post 4/5

```
It's native Android: Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Material 3, Room, DataStore, Hilt, Glance for the widget, and exact alarms for the reminders.

The full source code is on GitHub, MIT licensed:
https://github.com/jorgejiro/bebe-agua-android

If you're learning Compose or just curious, feel free to dig in.

4/5

#Kotlin #JetpackCompose #AndroidDev
```

### Post 5/5

```

https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.jjrapps.bebeagua

Missing a feature? Write to me at jjrmobileapps@gmail.com or open an issue on GitHub. I'll gladly review every request.

Boosts are very welcome, and stay hydrated! 💦

5/5
```
